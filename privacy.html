In short

RAKKA needs no account. Your scans, receipts, allergen guards and family profiles are stored on your device. They leave it only when you turn on a feature that needs the server, and section 3 lists exactly what is sent for each one. You can erase everything, on your device and on our server, with Delete my data in Settings.

1. What we do not collect

  • No account. No email, phone number, password or social login is needed to use RAKKA.
  • No location tracking. We do not collect your GPS position or your shopping routes.
  • No advertising or data sales. We do not sell, rent or share your dietary information, household profiles, watchlist or scan history with data brokers, insurers, food makers or advertisers. RAKKA has no ad or analytics trackers.

2. What stays on your device

RAKKA works offline first. These are saved in your device's browser storage and are not sent to us unless you turn on a feature in section 3:

  • Allergen guards and family profiles: names, avatars and allergen choices.
  • Watchlist and scan history: the products you scanned or imported from receipts.
  • Settings: theme, sound, haptics and filters.
  • Offline product database: a copy of public product information (names, brands and ingredient text), about 30 MB, so scans are fast and work without a signal. It holds nothing about you. You can turn it off and remove it in Settings, Privacy and data.

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Video is processed in memory on your device and is never recorded or uploaded.

4. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn alerts off: Settings, Background Recall Alerts. Your watchlist copy is erased from the server.
  • Delete my data: Settings, Delete my data. This erases your scans, receipts, profiles, settings and offline product database from your device, removes your background alert data from our server, leaves your household, and frees any invite code seat. If the server cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and you are told so, so you can try again.
  • Questions or requests: contact us at the address in section 8. Because RAKKA has no accounts, we identify your data by the random device ID in the app, so we can only act on requests made from the app itself.

5. Third-party services

  • Federal recall data: recalls come from the public openFDA and USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) feeds. The app requests them directly, and our server requests them to check alerts.
  • Google Gemini: reads receipt and lot-code photos, as described above.
  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode the app, or our server, asks this public database for the product name, brand and ingredients. Only the barcode is sent, and the request comes from your network address.
  • Fonts and code libraries: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts and its barcode and QR libraries from Cloudflare's cdnjs and jsDelivr when it opens, and its offline product list from GitHub Pages. These hosts see your network address as with any web request.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our server and storage.
  • Apple and Google push services: deliver the generic alert in the store app.
  • Subscriptions: Plus is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle payment. We do not receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm that a purchase is active. RevenueCat receives your RAKKA device ID and your purchase and subscription status, and the app store account that made the purchase. Plus can also be unlocked with an invite code while RAKKA is in testing.

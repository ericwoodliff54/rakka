Section 1

Not Medical Advice / No Healthcare Relationship

INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY; NO MEDICAL ADVICE.

The services, features, content, barcode lookups, receipt extractions, and scanning outputs provided by the App are for general informational, educational, and reference purposes only. The App is not a medical device, does not provide clinical or medical diagnoses, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, allergy testing, treatment, or judgment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or allergist regarding food allergies, dietary restrictions, anaphylaxis risks, or medical conditions. Never disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice because of information provided through the App. In the event of a suspected allergic reaction or medical emergency, immediately call emergency services (911 in the United States) or contact a local healthcare facility.

Section 2

Label Inspection & Inherent Scanning Limitations

MANDATORY PHYSICAL PACKAGE INSPECTION; SYSTEM LIMITATIONS.

You acknowledge and agree that:

1. Physical Label Controls: You must independently read, inspect, and verify the physical ingredient list, allergen warnings, and manufacturer statements directly on the physical packaging of every product prior to purchase, preparation, or consumption.

2. Scanning and OCR Fallibility: Optical character recognition (OCR), artificial intelligence vision models, receipt parsing, and camera image captures are inherently imperfect. Factors including glare, lighting, distorted text, wrinkled packaging, low resolution, or damaged barcodes may cause inaccurate, incomplete, or omitted readings.

3. Database Lags and Reformulation: Food manufacturers routinely reformulate recipes, change processing lines, alter facility allergen cross-contamination warnings, and modify packaging without notice. Product database entries, third-party API data, and automated feeds may be delayed, inaccurate, or out of date. We do not warrant that product data reflects the actual contents of the physical item in your hands.

FDA & Regulatory Recalls

THIRD-PARTY RECALL DATA LIMITATIONS.

The App aggregates publicly available recall, safety alert, and regulatory notice data (including feeds from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA)). We do not originate, verify, or guarantee the completeness, timeliness, or accuracy of regulatory agency notifications. A product’s absence from the App’s recall alerts does not guarantee that the product has not been recalled, contaminated, or subject to safety warnings. Always confirm active recalls directly with official government agencies (fda.gov / recalls.gov) and the product manufacturer.
